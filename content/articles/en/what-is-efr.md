Most emotional reactions are not random.

They are recurring patterns that automatically shape how we experience people, situations, and ourselves.

Emotional Factory Reset (EFR) is a practical method designed to help reduce the influence of these recurring emotional patterns rather than simply managing, suppressing, or reframing them.

The goal is not to teach you how to cope better with fear, anger, shame, guilt, rejection, jealousy, anxiety, resentment, or emotional pain.

The goal is to help these reactions lose their automatic grip so they no longer keep repeating.

Sessions are experiential, personalized, and focused on real emotional situations from your own life.

Suitable for individuals who repeatedly experience the same emotional triggers despite insight, self-awareness, therapy, or personal development.
